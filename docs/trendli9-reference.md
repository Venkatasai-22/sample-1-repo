hero with hold-to-press interaction, blueprint and sound controls, scroll-based
story beats, and routes to a quote and design studio. Its offering spans DTF
transfers, custom apparel, supplies and parts.

Use its product-first presentation and clear printing/customization paths as
direction for ColorFlu. Preserve ColorFlu branding, Bengaluru business details,
and the distinction between apparel and printing equipment. Do not copy its
contact details, pricing, testimonials, graphics, or technical claims.

scene. A reference-inspired interactive homepage is a separate implementation
from removing the opening video's concept labels. No homepage redesign is
claimed by this change.
